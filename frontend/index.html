03 / Multi-modal by design

OHRC × TMC-2 × IIRS × Kaguya/SELENE, LRO NAC

High-resolution optical → Medium-resolution optical → Hyperspectral → Lunar reference imagery from Kaguya/SELENE and LRO NAC. HELIOS preserves terrain structure and spectral information instead of treating every sensor as an ordinary image.

04 / Sun-angle aware

A shadow is not
a new feature.

HELIOS considers the observation and illumination conditions to distinguish stable lunar terrain structure from illumination-dependent appearance — enabling more reliable correspondence under substantially different solar conditions.

06 / Spatially uniform

More matches ≠ better registration.

HELIOS seeks high-quality correspondences distributed across the overlapping image, rather than allowing matches to cluster in a single textured region. Better distribution → Better geometry → More stable registration.

07 / Sub-pixel precision

From AI correspondence to precision registration.

Initial learned correspondences are locally refined to estimate precise feature locations. Target: sub-pixel registration accuracy. Every final correspondence can be accompanied by measurable confidence and residual error.

09 / Research system

Built for high-confidence lunar registration.

01 / FOUNDATION

Numerical core

Python and NumPy provide the coordinate, array, and structural calculations that hold the pipeline together.

02 / CORRESPONDENCE

Deep visual matching

DISK detects robust features while LightGlue forms fast, high-quality candidate correspondences.

03 / VALIDATION

Geometry & refinement

SIFT and FLANN provide a fallback; RANSAC verifies geometry, while phase correlation refines alignment to sub-pixel precision.

04 / RADIOMETRY

Illumination aware

A U-Net Generator and PatchGAN Discriminator help normalize radiometric variation before the terrain is compared.
